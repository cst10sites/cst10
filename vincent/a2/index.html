My Recipe: Homemade Egg Noodles

Introduction

  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 4large eggs
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Mix flour and salt together in a large bowl.
  2. beat eggs and pour them into the flour mixture to form dough.
  3. Knead the dough for 8 minutes until smooth, then let it rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll out the dough thinly and cut it into long noodle strips.
  5. Boil the noodles in hot water at 100°C until they float tothetop.
