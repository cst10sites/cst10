Beef noodle

Introduction

I will be making beef noodles, a delicious and filling dish made with tender beef, soft noodles, and a savory sauce. I chose this recipe because it is easy to prepare, tastes great, and combines protein and carbohydrates to make a satisfying meal. I also like that I can add vegetables for extra flavor and nutrition. I will use this Beef Lo Mein recipe as my original recipe reference.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (225 g) beef-thinly —sliced
  • 8 oz (225 g) egg noodlesor —lo mein noodles
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce—for savory flavor
  • 2 cloves garlic — minced
  • 1 cup broccoli —chopped
  • 1 medium carrot — thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil —for cooking the beef
  • 1 teaspoon sugar—to balance the sauce
  • 2 green onions—chopped for garnish

Operation

  • 1.Prepare the ingredients by thinly slicing the beef, cutting the vegetables, and chopping the cilantro.
  • 2.Brown the onion and ginger in a pot over medium-high heat until lightly browned.
  • 3.Add the garlic, beef broth, and water. Bring the mixture to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes to develop the flavor.
  • 4.Stir in the carrots, red bell pepper, soy sauce, and lime juice.
  • 5.Cook the rice noodles according to the package instructions, then drain them.
  • 6.Divide the noodles into serving bowls and place the thinly sliced beef on top.
  • 7.Pour the hot broth over the beef and noodles, then add the bean sprouts and cilantro as toppings. Serve hot and enjoy!
