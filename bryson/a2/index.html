Salmon and spring onions gratins

Introduction

This is a low calorie, light and healthy comfort meal. Made with common staple ingredients like canned salmon, spring onions and eggs. I chose this recipe since I've gotten more into more of a low calorie, high protein diet, and this recipe is amazing for it. I also personally love spring onions and salmon. Here is the original recipe.

Ingredients

  • 200g pink salmon (drained, skinned, and boned)
  • 2 spring onions (trimmed and finely sliced)
  • 4 small eggs
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Sea salt and black pepper to taste
  • Low-fat spray oil

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 180°C / 375°F.
  2. Grease two small, oven-proof dishes with the low-fat spray oil.
  3. Whisk the eggs in a bowl, season with salt and pepper, and stir in the lemon juice and sliced spring onions.
  4. Mash the drained salmon, gently fold it into the egg mixture, and spoon it into your prepared dishes.
  5. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until the gratins are puffed up, golden brown, and firm to the touch.
