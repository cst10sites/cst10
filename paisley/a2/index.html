How to make Truffle Fries

Introduction

I chose this recipe because I've loved French fries ever since I could remember. They've been my favourite food my entire life and are always good and really easy to make. When I was in around 4th grade, I made fries almost every single day after school. I've probably done over 5 projects on how to make fries, so I'd say I'm a pro. Anyways, my favourite fast food fries are here!

Ingredients

  • 1kg Potatos
  • 1.5-2L Vegetable Oil
  • 1 ½ tsp Salt
  • 1L Water
  • ½ tsp Black Pepper
  • ½ tsp Garlic Powder
  • ½ tsp Paprika

Instructions

  1. Wash, peel, and cut potatoes into even long strips.
  2. Soak potato sticks in cold water for 10 minutes.
  3. Drain and thoroughly dry potatoes.
  4. Fry the fries at 325°/165° C for 4-6 minutes, until pale and tender.
  5. Remove and rest the fries for 5-10 minutes.
  6. Increase oil temperature to 375° degrees/190° C.
  7. Put them back in the oil and fry them for 2 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  8. Drain on a paper towel and season immediately with salt.
