About me

With deep gratitude and respect, we are honoured to be learning and unlearning on the ancestral and unceded lands of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm (Musqueam), Sḵwxwú7mesh Úxwumixw (Squamish Nation) & səlilwətaɬ (Tsleil-Waututh Nation).

Hello! My name is River. I am creative and have a variety of interests, all tying into artwork in some shape or form. I love making things, specifically painting, drawing, and (my personal favorite) making figurines. I love playing the electric guitar, specifically metal music. Some bands I listen to include Slayer, Dying Fetus, Death, Metallica, Acid Bath, and Pantera.

This is my all time favorite website! I use it for the wayback machine, the free media, and the community behind it.

I took this course because I am very interested in the science of computers and making things. I have always wanted to be able to make my own websites and games, and I thought that taking this class would help me with that! So far, I have really enjoyed this class. I am excited to continue!
