My Recipe

Introduction

Welcome to this mediocre cookie recipe. These cookies will probably taste like a culinary mistake, and look like they were assembled during a minor earthquake. I can’t promise they’ll be edible, let alone enjoyable, but I can promise that a series of questionable decisions and a handful of ingredients will eventually produce something vaguely cookie-shaped.

Ingredients

  • 312 grams of all purpose flour
  • 226 grams of unsalted butter, softened
  • 100 grams of brown sugar
  • 50 grams of egg without shell
  • 5 grams of vanilla extract
  • 5 grams of baking soda
  • 4 grams of fine salt
  • An entire bag of chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Cream the butter and sugars Beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and white sugar for 2–3 minutes until light and fluffy.
  2. Add egg and vanilla Mix in the egg and vanilla until fully incorporated.
  3. Add the dry ingredients Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir them into the wet mixture just until combined, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Chill and portion Refrigerate the dough for 30 minutes. Divide into approximately 24 equal portions and place on parchment-lined baking sheets.
  5. Bake Preheat the oven to 177°C. Bake for 10–13 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centers remain slightly soft. Cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes.
  6. Boil a ½ cup of milk and enjoy your probably pretty mediocre cookies
