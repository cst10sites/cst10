(a) creates a hyperlink that takes users to another webpage Click here to go to next page
(em) makes text have empahsis you have to hurry!
(LI) creates a numbered list of items 1. first 2. second 3. third
(table) creates a table with rows and headings A table with sports results
(B) makes a text bold to show importance hello
(Ul) creates a bulleted list of items
    pizza
