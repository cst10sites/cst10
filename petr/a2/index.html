Oatmeal Recipe:

Introduction:

This is a very simple and delicious oatmeal recipe. It only takes a few minutes and doesn't require a lot of ingredients. I chose this recipe because I love oatmeal and I make it almost every morning!

Click here to find the original recipe!

Ingredients:

  • ½ Cup of old fashioned oats
  • 1 Cup of water or milk
  • Salt
  • Vanilla
  • Cinnamon
  • Berries of your choice

Step 1:

Combine ½ cup of oats, 1 cup of milk/water, salt, and vanilla in a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Bring the water/milk to a boil at 100°C .

Step 2:

Reduce the heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 2 to 5 minutes, or until the oatmeal is thick and creamy.

Step 3:

Remove from the heat and let stand for a minute before adding berries of your choice!

That’s it! Stovetop oatmeal is easy to make.
